Customer subscription detail, redrawn as a morning ledger

A side-by-side comparison of SubscriptionDetailScreen as it ships today and a proposed redesign. The shipped screen is reconstructed faithfully from source; the proposal applies the “Morning ledger” direction — warm paper, near-black ink, one amber accent reserved for money owed, and status carried by words and shape rather than colour alone.

What changed, and why

  1. P1 fixed Primary action is a full-width row; Skip and Pause sit in a 2-up grid with 60 px height; the 4th action moved under More actions. No label can clip at 328 px.
  2. P2 fixed The QR action now shows a labelled inline progress row (role="status"), not a silently greyed icon.
  3. P3 fixed Two distinct error surfaces: whole-screen load failure with Try again, and a separate history failure that says explicitly it is not an empty history.
  4. P4 fixed Empty state names the cause, shows start date / window / handover, and offers a single next step.
  5. P5 fixed Offline banner with cache timestamp; unavailable actions say why; queued action is visible and cancellable.
  6. P6 fixed One amount column, one rule: gross, prepaid credit, then Due now. Funding stated as “₹280 cash, every 7 deliveries”.
  7. P7 fixed Ink on paper ≥ 14:1; secondary ≥ 6.6:1; muted meta ≥ 5.7:1; on-brand text ≥ 6.4:1. Rail boundary on brand ≥ 6.4:1 (was 2.68:1).
  8. P8 fixed Back, More, Edit, View all, Cancel and every chip are real labelled controls; status badges pair a word with a glyph, so colour is never the only channel.
  9. P9 fixed Every control ≥ 46 px; text links are padded rows rather than bare 17 px text.
  10. P10 fixed Skeletons reserve the real layout height, so nothing jumps when data lands.
  11. P11 fixed Track is only primary while a delivery is live; jargon explained inline; handover mode stated in words with its consequence.
  12. P12 prefers-reduced-motion disables the shimmer, spinner and QR-busy animation. See proposal §7 for the RN follow-up.

The same screen at desktop width

The direction scales without becoming a different product: the ledger rail becomes a real navigation spine, the amount column stays a column, and the actions move to a single row that never wraps.
