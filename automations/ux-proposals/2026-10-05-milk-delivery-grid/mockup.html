Why this fits
  • Primary action first. “Dispatch route” is a full-width 44px button on the first screen — it never falls below the fold.
  • Money before the grid. The masthead gives litres, cash in and dues before any row, so the owner reads the balance first.
  • One column, one job. On phone the ledger keeps the subscriber margin and today’s column; the month is a horizontal scroll rather than a second layout.
  • Same controls, same labels. The accessible day button is identical on both widths.
  • No motion required. All transitions collapse under prefers-reduced-motion.
