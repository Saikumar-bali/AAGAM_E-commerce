Checkout, rebuilt as a market ledger

One screen, one job: turn a grocery cart into a paid order. The current screen buries the pay button under a full scroll of address and coupon chrome and shows money two different ways. This mockup puts the bill first, pins the action, and makes every failure state legible. Left = apps/mobile-customer/src/screens/customer/CheckoutScreen.tsx today. Right = proposed.

Direction: “Market Ledger” Warm receipt paper instead of cool grey · tabular money that sums in a column · serif for the amount you pay · one stamp-red total.

What moved

The bill and its total now render before payment and coupon, and the pay action is pinned to the bottom edge — visible on first paint at 360 dp, never more than a thumb away.

One money system

Every figure is ₹1,234.50 with tabular numerals, so the line items visibly sum to the total. The amount charged is the largest element on the screen.

Never a silent dead button

A reason line sits directly above the action and changes with the state: all-set, checking, retry-needed, or offline.

Accessibility

Persistent visible labels, 44 pt minimum targets, real Tab order with focus rings, errors at ~7.5:1 with an icon, and all motion gated behind prefers-reduced-motion.
