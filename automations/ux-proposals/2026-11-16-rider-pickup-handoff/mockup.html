Accessibility notes carried into the mockup. Every interactive element is a real <button>, <input> or <a> with a visible label or an aria-label; the step rail is an ordered list with aria-current="step"; the tie-out bar is an aria-live="polite" status; the error strip is role="alert"; the disabled CTA states its reason in text rather than only dimming; focus is drawn with :focus-visible; and @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) disables the skeleton shimmer. On React Native the equivalents are accessibilityRole, accessibilityState, accessibilityLiveRegion / AccessibilityInfo.announceForAccessibility, and AccessibilityInfo.isReduceMotionEnabled — see §5 of proposal.md for what still needs a device.

What this mockup cannot answer. Fold position on real hardware, TalkBack/VoiceOver traversal order, dynamic-type reflow at 200%, and whether the sticky footer correctly reserves content height are all listed as unverified in proposal.md §5. This file is a static comparison, not a runnable app.
