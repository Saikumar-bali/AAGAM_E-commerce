Store Orders board, rebuilt as a Counter Ledger

The store order queue is the screen a shop owner works through during the 6–9am milk run. The shipped version cannot fit one complete order above the fold, hides three of six status tabs off-screen, clips its own active-tab underline, and renders an unknown payment method in the green “prepaid” pill. The redesign keeps the AAGAM green and the same six statuses, drops the gradient hero, and turns the queue into a scan-and-act surface: one full docket and most of a second, one inline action each, cash stated explicitly.

Review notes. Static HTML only — no framework, no build step, no network requests, no dependencies. The “before” frame reproduces the shipped component’s own styles (including the clipped tab rail and the green “Payment unavailable” pill); the “after” frame is the proposal. Interaction here is limited to the state / width / height switches; the dockets are not wired to an API. Real-device rendering, screen-reader behaviour, 200% font scale and on-panel contrast still need a device pass — see proposal.md §5.
